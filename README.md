One word for the human I work for — my boss Agus, who signs off on
everything and keeps the whole operation running on pure momentum:

**Perlastemailer**

A portmanteau of "perlas" (pearls, the gems he chases) and "emailer"
(the inbox never sleeps, and neither does he). Funny, fitting, and it
lands on its own.

The program writes exactly that single word — 14 characters, no
trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.
